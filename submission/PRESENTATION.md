## Presentation Contents

The presentation covers:

- The problem and the need for sovereign, on-premise AI in confidential industrial environments
- The proposed Industrial AI Workbench solution
- Document generation, multimodal analysis, coding agents, agentic orchestration, audit logging, and local knowledge-base capabilities
- The system architecture and technology stack
- Feasibility, viability, risks, mitigation strategies, and deployment economics
- Economic, environmental, and social impact
- Technical references, LLM research areas, and confidentiality/compliance considerations
